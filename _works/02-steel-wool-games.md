As one of three engineers in the studio I got to work on a lot of VR projects in both Unity and Unreal Engine.

<img class="content-img-small" src="/images/work/steelwoolgames/bounce-header.jpg" loading="lazy">

[Bounce](https://store.steampowered.com/app/464400/Bounce/) was a physics-based VR puzzle game built in Unity. I was the lead developer from its early inception all the way up to launch in November 2016.

<img class="content-img-small" src="/images/work/steelwoolgames/mars-header.jpg" loading="lazy">

[Mars Odyssey](https://store.steampowered.com/app/465150/Mars_Odyssey/) was an educational VR experience built in Unreal Engine 4 and launched as both a steam title as well as a limited time experience at the Kennedy Space Center.

<img class="content-img-small" src="/images/work/steelwoolgames/rpo-header.jpg" loading="lazy">

[Ready Player One OASIS](https://store.steampowered.com/app/779650/Ready_Player_One_OASIS_beta/) was a unique VR game built in Unreal Engine 4 that launched alongside the movie Ready Player One. The game was well received for its wide variety of mini games.
