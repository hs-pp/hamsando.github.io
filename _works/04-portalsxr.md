PORTALSxr was a forward thinking mobile AR studio aiming to deliver apps that enhances events and festivals with augmented reality experiences.
We were a Unity engine shop shipping cool AR exeriences and utility apps for various clients.

- Supporting engineer for all of our contract AR apps.
- Lead Unity engineer developing R&D AR mobile apps for IOS and Android.
- Designed core platform architecture for the game systems used in our applications.
- Developed a number of Unity-Editor tools to support development workflows.
- Developed a custom AR framework used for rapid prototyping of AR experiences.
