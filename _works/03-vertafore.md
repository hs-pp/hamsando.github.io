Vertafore is a large insurance technology ("InsurTech") company that builds software platforms and infra for the insurance industry. Specifically the company excels at building software to improve workflows for agencies, carriers, MGAs (Managing General Agents), and brokers.

I decided I wanted to explore engineering outside of games to grow my breadth of knowledge and discover new interests. I still loved game dev but I was confident I could maintain the passion as a hobby.

With me I brought my excitement for learning new tech. In the first month I had already furiously drawn out some of the tech stack I'd be working in.

<img class="content-img-small" src="/images/work/vertafore/techstack.jpeg" loading="lazy">

My day to day involved writing Java with SpringBoot for business logic and then Javascript, React, and Redux for the frontend client. I wrote unit tests using Jest and Mockito for the backend and Junit and Enzyme for the frontend.

I was also the SCRUM master for a team size of 8 and held a smaller leadership role during a large portion of my time there.


Eventually I decided the enterprise engineering life was a bit too chill. I started yerning for the high speed, high impact lifestyle of game dev again and started searching for my next opportunity after only a year.
